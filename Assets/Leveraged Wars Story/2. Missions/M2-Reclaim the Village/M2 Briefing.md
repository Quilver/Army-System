# [[Bard.png|Bard]]
From distant roads, with but a faithful few,
Prince Julian returned unto his hearth;
The tyranny tax farmers wrought in greed
Brought great disquiet to his heavy heart.
# [[Bard.png|Bard]]
He wept to see a household starve for bread,
Their father called away to foreign war;
Their daughter Joan, wracked with whooping cough,
He healed at once with but a gentle touch.
# [[Bard.png|Bard]]
Yet sworn from birth unto his father's laws,
He planned to beg dear uncle Claudius
To chastise these black knights and stay their hand.
# [[Bard.png|Bard]]
Not till he saw the sack of Hira's shrine
Did he perceive that Pisco fooled the king.
# [[Bard.png|Bard]]
As loyal folk of fair Aurelliam,
Both men and women, rise at duty's call
To join Prince Julian in staunch defence
Against such wanton banditry; bring down
Their reign, and cast these thieves from out our town!
