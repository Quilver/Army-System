%% Tutorial 2 of 5. Plays as the Mission 1 battle starts (Start Battle pressed). Offer the pause so the player can try each order.
Cues: highlight the pause button and speed bar, a selected troop's card, a move path, the line ghost, a red attack path.
Controls come from SU-129, SU-156 and the Movie_022 review: click selects, double-click selects the chain, left-drag moves, right-drag makes a line, right-click on an enemy attacks, Alt leaves gaps, Ctrl + right-click faces. Box select comes from the sound plan. Check the pause control. %%

# [[Jagen.png|Jagen]]
The horn has sounded, my prince. Now you tell us what to do, and we shall do it. Mostly.

Pause the battle first, so you can think while I talk.

# [[Dark Prince.png|Prince Julian]]
I am paused. How do I take hold of a troop?

# Jagen
Click it. Its card appears. Click one of mine twice and you take the whole chain of us. Drag a box round several if you prefer.

# Prince Julian
And to make them walk?

# Jagen
Press on the troop and drag to where you want them. That is a plain Move. We walk there and plan nothing clever on the way.

# Prince Julian
I would like a proper line.

# Jagen
Right button, and drag. Where you press to where you let go is our front. Drag it long and we stand wide and thin. Drag it short and we stand narrow and deep.

The direction of the drag is the way we face.

# Prince Julian
Can several troops stand in one line?

# Jagen
Choose several, then drag. We stand shoulder to shoulder, and where our ends meet we link into a chain. A chain holds better than strangers do.

Hold Alt and we leave gaps and do not link.

# Prince Julian
And to attack?

# Jagen
Right-click the enemy troop. A red path means we are going to hit them, not walk past them. We shall pursue until it is finished, so choose your fight.

# Prince Julian
What if I only wish them to turn?

# Jagen
Hold Control and right-click. We shall face that way where we stand. Use it before the enemy reaches us, not after.

One more thing. A line wheels round. We do not spin on the spot, and we walk forward. Send us a long way sideways and we shall turn first and walk after.

# Prince Julian
Will they always obey?

# Jagen
Not if they have broken. A troop that is falling back, scattered or running will not hear you. Which is the lesson after this one.
