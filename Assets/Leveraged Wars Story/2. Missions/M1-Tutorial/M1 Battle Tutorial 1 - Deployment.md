Cues for the game: highlight the deployment zone, the deploy count, the enemy's wings, and the Start Battle button.

# [[Jagen.png|Jagen]]
A moment before the horn, my prince. Nobody has drawn steel yet.

This is the only time you may choose where we stand without an enemy shoving back. Spend it well.

# [[Dark Prince.png|Prince Julian]]
Then tell me, old friend. Where may I place them?

# Jagen
Within the marked ground. It is as far forward as we are allowed to set up.

Take hold of a troop and set it down where you want it.

# Prince Julian
Surely I may bring every sword we have.

# Jagen
No, my prince. Each battle allows only so many, and whoever stays behind sits this one out.

Choose the ones who will earn their place.

# Prince Julian
And what of the enemy?

# Jagen
Look across the field. See their horsemen on the wing?

Horsemen do not charge a line head on. They ride round and strike from behind. Mind that when you set ours.

# Prince Julian
Then where do my own riders go?

# Jagen
Out on the edge, where they have room to ride. Better you ride round them first than they ride round us.

Spears in the middle, where the fighting will be. Archers behind or beside, never in front. And nothing with its back to the enemy.

# Prince Julian
How will I know when I am done?

# Jagen
When you stop wanting to move anyone.

Then press Start Battle. The horn sounds, and after that you may give orders and nothing more. You cannot pick a troop up and carry it.

# Prince Julian
Then let us hope I am a quick learner.
