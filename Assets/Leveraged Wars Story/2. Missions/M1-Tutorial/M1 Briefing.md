*Introduction to Julian's Backstory*
# [[Bard.png|Bard]]
When the vile warlord Hartkan seized power, young Prince Julian was forced to flee for his life at the tender age of four. Accompanied by his loyal knight, they escaped Candera and sought refuge in the free city of Majula.
Having lost his family, Julian was groomed to reclaim the throne. He learned the ways of the sword, the art of war, politics, manners, and charm. Most importantly, he delved into the world of banking, recognizing the power that debt could wield in achieving seemingly impossible goals...
With Hartkan's forces away from Majula, Julian sees a prime opportunity to reclaim his homeland. Gathering his exiled supporters, all he needs is a seed loan to initiate the liberation of Majula.
