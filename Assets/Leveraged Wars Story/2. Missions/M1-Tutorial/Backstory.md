# [[Bard.png|Bard]]
Hear ye, hear ye! Attend the noble tale
# [[Bard.png|Bard]]
Of prince Julian, 
To good King Prinius and Freya born,
True heir to all Aurelliam's high throne.
# [[Bard.png|Bard]]
The royal hearth bestowed its richest gifts:
Their noble spirit, beauty, health, and grace;
And at his very birth, the falcon sang.
# [[Bard.png|Bard]]
He grew in strength; all beasts within the realm
Gave heed unto their prince. The fiercest boars
Of all Aurelliam came meek as lambs
Upon his maiden hunt; and where he pissed,
The sweetest roses burst in sudden bloom.
# [[Bard.png|Bard]]
He would have stayed among us, grown to rule,
A king both hale and wise, whose gentle touch
Had healed the sick throughout our troubled land.
We weep for Prinius and Freya slain,
Deceived, alas, by black betrayal's snare.
That poison, steeped in envy, struck them dead,
And had undone young Julian as well,
Had not the Faeries whisked him safe away.
# [[Bard.png|Bard]]
Within Queen Guenivere's bright faery court,
With hermits of a thousand years he learned,
He diced with Death, and slew with naked hands
A serpent rising full a hundred feet.
Yet left the fairest princess of that shore,
# [[Bard.png|Bard]]
To seek Aurelliam, his home, once more.
