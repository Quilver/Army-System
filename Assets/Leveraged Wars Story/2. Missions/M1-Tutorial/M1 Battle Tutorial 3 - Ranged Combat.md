%% Tutorial 5 of 5 by topic, but it plays THIRD in Mission 1: the approach is under archer fire. Plays on the first volley that is fired at a player troop.
Cues: the green range cone, the Shock fill rising on a troop under fire, a hit and a miss.
Check against spec: whether an attack order walks archers into range, which missiles fly flat or arc, and line of sight (SU-136; the mission workflow plan says no line-of-sight rule exists yet). Lines marked CHECK are the ones that depend on this. %%

# [[Jagen.png|Jagen]]
Hear that, my prince? Bows.

# [[Dark Prince.png|Prince Julian]]
Their archers have found us. Are we losing men already?

# Jagen
Some, but that is not what the arrows are for. Most of what archers do is frighten.

Every arrow that lands piles on Shock. A troop that marches in under fire arrives half broken before the lines even meet.

# Prince Julian
Do arrows always wound?

# Jagen
No. Mail and shields turn them. Against men in plain cloth they bleed.

# Prince Julian
Can I use archers too?

# Jagen
See the green cone? That is where they can reach. Inside it they shoot without being told. Outside it they will not. Order them forward and they will walk into range before they loose. CHECK

# Prince Julian
Is there a place they cannot shoot?

# Jagen
Too close. They need room, like a swordsman. An enemy on top of them is a problem a bow cannot solve, and in melee an archer is no better than a farmer.

# Prince Julian
Can they shoot over our own men?

# Jagen
Arrows climb and come down. Flat shots do not. Keep your own troops out of the line of fire where you can. CHECK

Javelins run dry, too, so do not waste them.

# Prince Julian
Then where should they stand?

# Jagen
Behind a line of spears that will hold, or to the side where they have a clear view. Keep them away from horsemen. And once the lines meet, turn them on something else.

# Prince Julian
Then I know what to do about theirs. Ride them down.

# Jagen
Quickly, my prince. Archers hate being caught.
