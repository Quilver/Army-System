# ![Mentor](<Assets/Temporary Assets/Characters/Special Character/Knight.png>) {right}
You can move the camera using the right stick or WASD. You can zoom in and out with the triggers or the QE keys

To select a unit you can toggle with the bumpers or left click on the unit you want to select.

To move the unit you have selected move the cursor to location you want it to move to and hit the A button the gamepad or right click. If you have the cursor on enemy unit then you will be ordering your unit to attack it.
