%% Tutorial 4 of 5 by topic. Plays on the first contact between a player troop and an enemy troop (Engaged). It uses the nerve and Shock terms that the Morale scene introduces; if the Morale scene is triggered later than first contact, swap the order or trigger this one on the first flank or rear hit instead.
Cues: the heat band along the contact, the four faces (front, left, right, rear), a "Flanked!" call-out, a right-click on an attacker, an enemy closing on the rear.
Assault: soldiers in the front rank charge together at the start of contact (SU-175, SU-183). The attacker wheels to square up and the defender keeps its facing (Movie_022 review). %%

# [[Jagen.png|Jagen]]
Here it comes, my prince. Watch our front rank.

Once we touch, the first soldiers go in all together. That is the Assault. It hits hardest at the start. After that it is one man against another, duel by duel.

# [[Dark Prince.png|Prince Julian]]
Why do they turn along the line of the enemy?

# Jagen
To get the whole front onto them. The more of us touching, the more of us fighting.

The troop that attacks turns to meet the other along its length. The troop being attacked stays as it is. Mind that.

# Prince Julian
Why does that matter?

# Jagen
Because a troop has four faces. Front, two sides and back.

Hit our front and we fight as usual. Hit our side or our back and the Shock triples. That is how a few good blows break a large troop.

# Prince Julian
So my riders should go round behind them.

# Jagen
That is the prince's trick, and a good one. We hold them in front, you ride round behind, and a whole line folds. We call it hammer and anvil.

The enemy knows it as well as we do. See that call-out? One of ours has been hit from the side.

# Prince Julian
How do I answer a flank?

# Jagen
Turn the troop on whoever is hitting it. Right-click the attacker and we shall swing to face him.

It will not undo the first blow, but it stops the rest landing on our back.

# Prince Julian
And what is Surrounded?

# Jagen
Look behind us. If an enemy stands where we would fall back to, and another is in front, we cannot give ground. Every break then costs us more.

Never fight with your back to the enemy and nowhere to go.

# Prince Julian
How do I avoid it?

# Jagen
Keep a reserve behind the line. Keep your chain tight so there are no open sides in the middle. The ends are the weak points, so cover them or have a troop ready to answer.

# Prince Julian
Hold them, old friend. I shall take the back.
