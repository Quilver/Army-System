# [[Jagen.png|Jagen]]
Steady, my prince. There are two kinds of health in this army. Learn to tell them apart.

# [[Dark Prince.png|Prince Julian]]
Go on.

# Jagen
Soldiers first. Hover over one and you will see his own bar. Armour matters. A blow that bounces off mail does nothing. A bruise stings and mends. A cut bleeds, and bleeding is what kills.

# Prince Julian
Then I win by killing them all.

# Jagen
No. Look at the banner over the troop. That is its nerve. Every living soldier adds to it, and every man lost takes some away.

See the pale fill creeping across it? That is Shock. Each hit that lands piles some on, and a hit from the side or the rear piles on triple.

The white tick is the troop's breaking point. When Shock reaches it, the troop breaks.

# Prince Julian
What does breaking look like?

# Jagen
It depends where you stand.

Caught in melee with room behind you, you fall back. That is a Retreat. It costs you some nerve, but you live.

Broken while not in melee, say under arrows, you are in Disorder and cannot be ordered.

If an enemy stands where you would fall back to, you cannot. You are Surrounded. That costs the most, and you do not even get to leave.

# Prince Julian
And after a break?

# Jagen
You are dazed. See the stars? A few moments in which a troop cannot charge, cannot be ordered and cannot hit back. It is when an enemy finishes you.

# Prince Julian
And if the nerve is gone altogether?

# Jagen
If a troop has bled down to about half the nerve it started with, it routs. It runs, it scatters, and it leaves the field. Nothing you say will turn it.

# Prince Julian
Even with every man alive?

# Jagen
Aye. Nerve is not flesh, my prince.

# Prince Julian
Then I must watch the banners.

# Jagen
Pull a troop back before the fill reaches the tick. Keep a reserve behind the line so we have somewhere to fall to. And remember, you do not win by killing the enemy. You win by making them break.
